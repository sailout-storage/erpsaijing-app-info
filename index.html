01

Overview

ERPSAIJING is an internal integration tool under development for our company’s authorized Allegro seller accounts.

It is intended to retrieve orders, offer and product information, and account billing data through the Allegro REST API to support operational reporting and business analysis. Actual access depends on the permissions granted by each seller account and the API resources available.

02

Intended data scope

Orders

Authorized order records for sales reporting and operational analysis.

Offers & products

Seller offer and related product information for internal reporting.

Account billing

Available account charges and fee records for cost analysis.

03

Authorization & use

The intended integration uses seller consent through Allegro OAuth and read permissions appropriate to these reporting needs. It is designed for the company’s own authorized accounts and internal use.

This page documents the application’s purpose. It does not provide a seller login, collect API credentials, or display shop data. It does not claim that the integration is live or certified by Allegro.

中文说明

ERPSAIJING 是公司正在开发的内部数据集成工具，面向公司已授权的 Allegro 店铺账号。

应用计划通过 Allegro REST API 读取订单、商品与刊登信息、账户费用数据，用于运营报表和经营分析。实际可读取的数据，以店铺授予的权限及平台开放的接口为准。

当前处于开发阶段，本说明不代表已完成平台接入、已实现向领星 ERP 写入数据，或已获得 Allegro 官方认证。
